About

Open Peru began as a course project in the University of Chicago’s Master of Computational Analysis and Public Policy program. It has since been developed as a public data pipeline for legislative information, collecting official public records from the Peruvian Congress, processing them, and organizing them into a structured, clean relational database.

Use the platform to explore how bills move through Congress, who sponsors them, and when their text changes over time.

1

Search a bill

Find bills by title, author, committee, date, status, or whether they have differences between versions.

2

Review its path

Open a bill to see its timeline: presentation, committee review, votes, archival decisions, approval, and text revisions.

3

Explore who is behind it

Move from a bill to its author, party, committee memberships, recent bills, and approval metrics.
